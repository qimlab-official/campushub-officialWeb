Global Architecture

Truly Cross-Campus Compatible

A student's life isn't confined to one location. Our architecture allows seamless roaming between any campus in the Campus Hub network.

  • Universal Wallet & Rewards

    Earn points at your home campus, spend them at a Nexo POS mini-mart in a partnered university across town.

  • Single Identity

    One app login rules them all. Access market places and cafes instantly wherever the ecosystem exists.

Postal Hub Restriction While wallets and IDs roam freely, the Postal Management System remains strictly localized. Parcels are processed only at the specific campus Hub matching the delivery address.
